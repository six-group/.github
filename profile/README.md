We operate the [Swiss](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/the-swiss-stock-exchange.html) and [Spanish](https://www.bolsasymercados.es/) stock exchanges, process billions of transactions, and deliver real-time market data and payment infrastructure across Europe. ## About SIX

SIX provides and operates stable, efficient infrastructure for financial markets across Switzerland, the EU, and the UK. We are the backbone of the Swiss financial center and the third-largest financial market infrastructure provider in Europe by market cap.
